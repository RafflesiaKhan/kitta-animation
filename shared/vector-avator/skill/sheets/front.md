[What to draw in this picture: the front standing pose (1 figure)]

On a portrait picture, draw only one character, full body and large. From the top of the head (including ear and hair tips) to the soles fits in about 85% of the picture height, with margins on all four sides.

The pose is the basic reference pose.
- Body and face look straight ahead. The gaze too. Do not tilt or twist the body.
- Stand straight with the feet slightly apart.
- Both arms hang down, spread about 30 to 40 degrees diagonally down from the shoulders (A-pose). Elbows lightly straight, hands lightly open mittens with the palms towards the body. Leave a gap between armpit and torso.
- A tail and large ears or hair sit in a natural position, not hidden too much behind the body.
- A calm normal expression, the mouth a small closed smile.

No text, labels, symbols, shadows or floor.
