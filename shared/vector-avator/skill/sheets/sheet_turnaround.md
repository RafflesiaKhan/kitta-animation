[What to draw in this picture: character sheet 1 (turnaround and hand shapes)]

Draw one landscape character sheet. Every figure is the same character, at the same size, in the same style.

Top row (about the upper 70% of the picture): four full-body views, evenly spaced in one row from left to right. All four have their soles at the same height and the same head size.
1. Front: facing straight ahead.
2. 3/4: body and face turned about 45 degrees towards the left of the picture (the face looks to the left of the picture, and the character's left half is nearer).
3. Side: exact profile facing the left of the picture.
4. Back: seen from straight behind.
All four use the same basic reference pose. Stand straight with the feet slightly apart, both arms hanging down spread about 30 to 40 degrees diagonally down from the shoulders (A-pose), with a gap between armpit and torso. A calm normal expression. Do not tilt or twist the body.

Bottom row (about the lower 30% of the picture): four shapes of the character's right hand, only from the wrist on and fairly large, in one row from left to right. Close the cut at the wrist with an ellipse in the arm colour.
1. Open hand (showing the back of the hand)
2. Pointing with the index finger (the fingertip points to the left of the picture)
3. Fist
4. Hand held out with the palm up
The size and shape of the hands match the hands of the full-body figures above.

Under each figure, put a small English label: front, 3/4, side, back, open, point, fist, palm. No other text, titles, frames, arrows or colour palettes.
