[What to draw in this picture: character sheet 2 (face, eyes, mouths, expressions)]

Draw one landscape character sheet. Every figure is the head, from the shoulders up, of the same character. All heads are drawn at the same size and in the same style, evenly spaced in a 3-row grid. Even with long ears or hair, leave enough space that the heads do not overlap.

Row 1 (5 figures), from the left:
1. Front face (normal)
2. Face at about 45 degrees (a 3/4 view with the face turned towards the left of the picture; normal expression)
3. Front face: eyes open (normal)
4. Front face: half-closed eyes (the upper lid hides the upper half of the iris; do not flatten the shape of the iris)
5. Front face: eyes closed (a gentle downward arc)

Row 2 (6 figures): front face, changing only the mouth. The eyes stay open and normal. From the left:
1. Closed mouth
2. "a" (wide open both vertically and horizontally)
3. "i" (wide horizontally, narrow vertically)
4. "u" (a small pursed circle)
5. "e" (wide horizontally, medium opening)
6. "o" (a tall oval)

Row 3 (4 figures): front face, changing the expression. From the left:
1. Normal
2. Smile (eyes narrowed, mouth open in a laugh)
3. Surprised (eyes wide and round, brows raised, mouth a small round opening)
4. Troubled (outer ends of the brows lowered, mouth small and wavy)

Under each figure, put a small English label: front, 3/4, open, half, closed, closed mouth, a, i, u, e, o, normal, smile, surprised, troubled. No other text, titles, frames or arrows. For a character without brows, tell the expressions apart by the shapes of the eyes, lids and mouth.
