[What to draw in this picture: an existing character redrawn in the common style (front standing pose, 1 figure)]

Redraw the character of the attached source picture (and character sheet) in the common style above. The character design (colours, shapes of ears, hair, clothes and tail, eye shape and colour, placement of the face features, impression of the body) follows the source picture; only the way of drawing (proportions, fills, boundary lines, eyes) follows the common style. Do not use the source's thick black outlines; replace them with thin lines of the same hue.

On a portrait picture, draw only one character, full body and large. From the top of the head (including ear and hair tips) to the soles fits in about 85% of the picture height.

The pose is the basic reference pose.
- Body and face look straight ahead. The gaze too. Do not tilt or twist the body.
- Stand straight with the feet slightly apart.
- Both arms hang down, spread about 30 to 40 degrees diagonally down from the shoulders (A-pose). Elbows lightly straight, hands lightly open with the palms towards the body. Leave a gap between armpit and torso.
- A calm normal expression, the mouth a small closed smile.

No text, labels, symbols, shadows or floor.
