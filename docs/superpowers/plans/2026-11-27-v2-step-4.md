## Required sequence

Each task gets failing tests first, implementation, full Vitest and TypeScript before its commit, then a fresh read-only review and a fix/recheck pass. The coordinator performs the planner and reviewer stages locally because the planner subagent was unavailable. No Director, transitions, props, walking, new actions, or other providers.
